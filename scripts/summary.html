H₂ Ground-State Dissociation Curve

Simulation plan summary

Problem Statement

Determine the desired accuracy or level of theory for the H₂ ground-state dissociation curve (quick qualitative, good quantitative, or near-benchmark), and identify the intended compute environment (laptop/workstation or HPC cluster, including scheduler or GPU availability if applicable).

System & Goals

  • System: isolated H₂ molecule
  • Target: ground-state dissociation / potential-energy curve as a function of bond length
  • Primary objective: obtain a physically correct and smooth dissociation curve across equilibrium and stretched-bond regions
  • Accuracy options to decide: qualitative, quantitative, or near-benchmark
  • Execution context to decide: local workstation/laptop or HPC resources
  • Key physics concern: correct treatment of static correlation at large bond separation

Technical Recommendations

Area Recommendation
Package Use ORCA or PySCF for molecular benchmark work. Use VASP or Quantum ESPRESSO only if a periodic plane-wave workflow is specifically required.
DFT Functional Use PBE0 or B3LYP for good quantitative curves. Avoid plain GGA-only DFT for stretched-bond H₂ because semilocal functionals mishandle static correlation.
Near-Benchmark Theory Use CCSD(T) near equilibrium, but switch to CASSCF/CAS(2,2) or FCI for full dissociation benchmarking, since single-reference methods break down at large bond stretch.
Basis Set def2-TZVP for quick qualitative work; def2-QZVPP or aug-cc-pVQZ for good quantitative results; aug-cc-pV5Z or CBS extrapolation for near-benchmark accuracy.
Dispersion No empirical dispersion correction is needed for intramolecular H₂ dissociation.
Spin Treatment Use unrestricted KS/UKS only as a diagnostic. Prefer restricted methods near equilibrium and multireference treatment at large separation. Watch for spin-symmetry breaking.
Convergence Use tight thresholds, e.g. SCF convergence around 1e-8 Ha and total-energy tolerance around 1e-6 Ha, because the curve depends on small energy differences.
MLIP Use MLIP is not recommended as the primary tool for H₂ dissociation. Direct electronic-structure calculations are cheaper and more reliable for this 2-atom system.
ML Surrogate Option If a surrogate is desired, fit a small supervised model directly to ab initio energies and forces rather than using a large foundation MLIP.
Training Set Use about 30–100 geometries spanning 0.4–8.0 Å, with denser sampling near equilibrium and in the 1.5–4.0 Å stretch region.
Training Targets Train on energies and forces; if possible, include dissociation energy and equilibrium bond constraints during validation.
Finetuning Skip foundation-model finetuning. For a small fitted model, use a low learning rate (~1e-3 to 1e-4) and early stopping to reduce overfitting risk.
Validation Check size consistency at large separation against 2 × E(H atom); compare restricted vs unrestricted curves and inspect <S²>; recompute selected points with a larger basis and multireference reference.

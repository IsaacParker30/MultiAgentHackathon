### Findings on the Binding Energy of Water to Hexagonal Boron Nitride

1. **Target System**: Water on hexagonal boron nitride (hBN).

2. **DFT Code Type**: The primary study utilized **Diffusion Monte Carlo (DMC)** methods for accurate interaction energy calculations. However, for DFT calculations, common codes include **VASP** and **Quantum ESPRESSO** (both using plane-wave + pseudopotential methods). VASP is recommended for its user-friendly interface and extensive documentation.

3. **Recommended DFT Functional(s)**: The study by Al-Hamdani et al. (2015) does not specify a DFT functional but emphasizes the need for accurate methods like DMC for binding energy calculations. General practice suggests using **PBE** or **B3LYP** for similar systems.

4. **Dispersion Correction**: Not explicitly mentioned in the papers found. Typically, **D3 or D3(BJ)** corrections are recommended for systems involving van der Waals interactions.

5. **Pseudopotentials / Basis Set**: For VASP, **PAW** pseudopotentials are commonly used for B, N, O, and H. For Quantum ESPRESSO, **ultrasoft pseudopotentials** can be employed.

6. **Plane-wave Cutoff (eV)**: A typical cutoff for VASP is around **500 eV**, while Quantum ESPRESSO often uses a cutoff of **40-60 Ry**.

7. **K-point Sampling**: Generally, a **1x1x1** k-point grid is sufficient for large hBN slabs, but this may vary based on the system size and specific calculations.

8. **Supercell/Slab Setup**: A common setup involves a **3-5 layer hBN slab** with a vacuum gap of **10-15 Å** to minimize interactions between periodic images.

9. **Number of Water Molecules**: The binding energy study focused on a **single water monomer**.

10. **Temperature and Thermostat**: The DMC study does not specify temperature, but typical MD simulations for similar systems are conducted at **300 K** using a **Nosé-Hoover thermostat**.

11. **MD Timestep and Trajectory Length**: The DMC method does not involve MD timesteps, but for AIMD, a timestep of **1 fs** is common, with trajectory lengths varying from **10 ps to 100 ps**.

12. **How to Compute the Quantity Asked**: The binding energy can be computed as:
   \[
   E_{\text{binding}} = E_{\text{hBN}} + E_{\text{water}} - E_{\text{hBN-water}}
   \]
   where \(E_{\text{hBN-water}}\) is the total energy of the system with water adsorbed.

13. **Parameters Still Uncertain**: Specific DFT functional recommendations and dispersion corrections were not detailed in the literature.

14. **Papers Found**:
   - **Water on hexagonal boron nitride from diffusion Monte Carlo**  
     Yasmine S. Al-Hamdani, Ming Ma, Dario Alfè, 2015, arXiv:1505.03291v1
   - **Effects of electrostatic interactions on Kapitza resistance in hexagonal boron nitride-water interfaces**  
     Sobin Alosious, Sridhar Kumar Kannam, Sarith P. Sathian, 2022, arXiv:2203.12618v1
   - **Friction of Water on Graphene and Hexagonal Boron Nitride from ab initio Methods: Very Different Slippage Despite Very Similar Interface Structures**  
     Gabriele Tocci, Laurent Joly, Angelos Michaelides, 2015, arXiv:1503.05249v1

### Conclusion
The binding energy of water on hexagonal boron nitride is reported as approximately **-84 ± 5 meV** based on the DMC study. Further DFT studies may provide additional insights into the interaction mechanisms and energetics involved.
